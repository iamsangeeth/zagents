# Parikzan Content Guidelines

## Website

- Approved website URL: `https://www.parikzen.com`
- Display domain may be written as `www.parikzen.com`.
- Use this exact domain. Do not invent paths, query parameters, pricing pages, or product routes.

## Quiz CTA policy

Every article must evaluate whether a quiz CTA helps the reader. CTA is optional, not automatic.

Include one natural CTA when the article is about learning, study, assessment, quizzes, knowledge checks, or a closely related topic. Prefer CTA near the conclusion or after a useful section:

> If you want more practice, give your topic or notes/file to AI to generate a quiz at [www.parikzen.com](https://www.parikzen.com).

Use `quiz_cta` field when structured output supports it. Keep CTA to one short paragraph or at most two sentences, place it near the conclusion, and make it optional. Do not repeat CTA or mention Parikzen elsewhere unless source-backed context requires it.

Omit CTA when topic is unrelated or CTA would feel forced, misleading, or disruptive. Never claim that a quiz was generated, completed, scored, or available at a specific route unless supplied context confirms it.

## Content safety

- Do not invent website capabilities or destination pages.
- Do not include tracking parameters unless explicitly supplied.
- Do not turn optional CTA into a requirement when article context does not support it.
- Keep one clear CTA maximum per article.

## Editorial strategy

- Write for the reader's problem first. Article must teach a general, useful skill such as exam preparation, revision, self-evaluation, active recall, or assessment design.
- Keep at least 90% of article body general and actionable. Reader should get complete value without visiting Parikzen.
- Do not make Parikzen the topic, solution, title, H1, introduction, primary keyword, or a required step. Do not write product tutorials, credit-saving articles, feature explainers, or product-led comparisons as general education articles.
- Mention Parikzen only as one optional suggestion near the conclusion, after the useful guidance is complete. Keep it to one short paragraph or at most two sentences; never repeat the brand throughout the article.
- When relevant, CTA may explain that readers can give AI a topic, prompt, or notes/file to generate a practice quiz. Use only capabilities supported by `knowledge/PRODUCT.md`.
- A reader who ignores CTA must still have a complete plan they can apply immediately.
